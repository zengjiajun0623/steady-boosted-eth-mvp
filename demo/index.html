FAQ

Common questions

What am I buying?

Steady ETH and Boosted ETH are ETH exposure tokens. You are changing the risk profile of your ETH, not buying a dollar stablecoin.

Why can I only trade with ETH?

The simple MVP flow is ETH in and ETH out. If someone starts with USDC, they can swap to ETH first, then choose Steady ETH or Boosted ETH.

What does the vault do?

LPs deposit ETH into the protocol liquidity engine. The vault makes Steady ETH and Boosted ETH tradeable, and it earns trading fees plus roll and inventory PnL.

Who fills roll auctions?

External solvers can bid directly to improve execution. At small launch caps, the ETH LP vault can also clear rolls after a short solver-first window.

What is the main vault risk?

The vault can underperform simply holding ETH if inventory or roll pricing moves against it. Returns are not guaranteed.

How is settlement priced?

Final pricing uses the median of USDC, USDT, and DAI Uniswap TWAPs, so one stablecoin source cannot set settlement by itself.
